01 — About

A creative
generalist
by design

Saravanan is a multidisciplinary creative professional working across writing, creative direction, show production, content creation, video editing and AI-generated video.

Over five years he has moved through television, YouTube, digital media, events, advertising and political campaigns — always sitting at the point where an idea turns into something an audience can watch, read or feel.

His process runs the full distance from concept to delivery: developing the idea, writing the script, shaping the visuals, producing the show, editing the footage and, increasingly, generating and directing AI video as part of that same craft. He treats AI as another instrument in the process, not a replacement for the storytelling and direction underneath it.
